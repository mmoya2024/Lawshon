What the Curriculum Covers

Every class blends theory, yard practice and road training, following the NTSA driving school curriculum units.

📘 Theory Instruction

  • Vehicle construction, controls and basic mechanics
  • Self-inspection of vehicles before driving
  • Highway Code — road signs, signals and markings
  • Traffic laws, regulations and driver responsibilities
  • Defensive driving and hazard perception
  • Emergency procedures and accident response
  • Class-specific rules (PSV conduct, goods documentation, plant safety, etc.)
  • Internal theory assessment before practicals begin

🚗 Practical Training

  • Vehicle control or manoeuvre yard: reversing, parking, hill starts, three-point turns
  • Clutch, gear, braking and steering control
  • Supervised road driving in varied traffic conditions
  • Night and highway driving practice (class-dependent)
  • Coupling/uncoupling and load handling (C, D, E classes)
  • Passenger safety and PSV conduct on-road (D classes)
  • Equipment operation drills (G class)
  • Mock test, readiness assessment and NTSA test booking

Enrollment to License, Step by Step

Register

National ID, passport photo and any class-specific documents (e.g. Certificate of Good Conduct for PSV).

Interim License

We help you apply for your interim/learner's license via NTSA before lessons begin.

Theory & Yard

Classroom sessions and manoeuvre-yard practice for your specific class.

Road Training

Supervised on-road lessons until you meet our internal readiness standard.

NTSA Test

We book your official NTSA test and accompany you on test day.

Note: NTSA test booking, interim license and Smart Driving License fees are paid directly to NTSA via eCitizen and are separate from our tuition fees. Professional and heavier subclasses (B3, C, D, E) require proof of a minimum number of years' driving experience on the prerequisite class, per NTSA rules — bring your current license when registering.
